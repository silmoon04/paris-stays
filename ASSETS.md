# Paris Stays assets

The primary mark was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized to a 192px transparent PNG for delivery. Its source is `src/assets/paris-stays-mark.png`; Vite publishes a hashed copy used by the header, startup screen, favicon and phone home-screen icon. The full-resolution original remains private.

## Final logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: primary app logo mark for Paris Stays, a polished family accommodation search app with white surfaces, charcoal text and a restrained rose accent. Create one professional, original flat geometric emblem that combines a Parisian arched doorway with a subtle P monogram and a simple roofline. Strong clear silhouette, balanced rounded geometry, confident even strokes, refined hospitality identity. One solid rose color #E83159. Mark only, no wordmark or letters outside the emblem, no slogan. Center a single emblem with tight safe padding on a genuinely transparent square canvas. It must remain clear at 32 pixels, so avoid tiny windows, fine detail, landmarks collages, decorative flourishes, shadows, gradients, mockups, paper textures, borders, multiple concepts or watermark. Deliver a clean production-ready transparent logo asset.

## Outfit

The 100–900 variable Latin WOFF2 is self-hosted at `src/assets/outfit-latin-variable.woff2`, preloaded and used with `font-display: swap`. Google Fonts is only the source used during development; visitors load the font from this site.

Source: [Outfit on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit), [original font repository](https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts). The SIL Open Font License is included at `src/assets/Outfit-OFL.txt` and copied into the published assets directory.
